# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026/04/06**

Your privacy is important. Here's how we process your data on this portfolio and MVP site:

---

## 1. Data Collected

When you use the site, we only collect the personal information necessary for authentication and tracking your activity:

### Auth App (User)

- **Email**
- **First Name / Last Name**
- **Password** (securely stored, hashed)
- **Last Login** (`last_login`)

### Tracking App (UserProgram, UserProgramSession, UserProgramFeedback)

- Tracking of programs and sessions you attend
- Feedback on exercises/programs
- Status and progress notes

> UUIDs, timestamps (`created_at`, `updated_at`), and program/exercise data (slug, images, content, translations) are not considered personal data unless they are linked to an identified user.

---

## 2. Use of Data

Your data is used solely for:

- Authentication and management of your account
- Tracking of your programs, sessions, and feedback
- Personalized display of your profile

> We never share your personal data with third parties.

--

## 3. Cookies

The site uses cookies for:

- Secure authentication via session or JWT token
- Maintaining your login

> These cookies are strictly necessary for the site to function and do not contain any personal data readable by the browser.

--

## 4. Security

- Passwords are hashed using Django mechanisms by default.

- Authentication cookies are HTTPOnly and secure when the site is using HTTPS.

- We implement best security practices to protect your data.

## 5. Your Rights

In accordance with the GDPR, you have the right to:

- Request the correction of your data

> To exercise your rights, contact the website administrator via the email address provided on the website.

## 6. Data Retention Period

- Personal data is retained as long as your account exists.

- Once your account is deleted, all your personal information is deleted.
